I am a draftsperson with an obsession for detail and a strong belief that design should be as accurate as it is beautiful. I see myself as a technician with a designer’s mindset, someone who appreciates not just the functionality of technology, but also the beauty and possibilities it offers.

I don’t believe in limitations; I see opportunities for innovation. When something seems difficult, I take it as a sign that there may be a better way to approach it.
