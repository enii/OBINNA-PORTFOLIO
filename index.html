Construction process

Loose side and perspective sketches set the flow of the frame: one sweeping line from temple tip to lens. The strongest ideas were then built on standard head sizes to check fit, coverage and wrap.

Process

The final idea was drawn to scale, then modelled in 3D as a sculpted temple and lens chassis joined by an exposed hinge.

Moodboard

Named after the stingray: a flat, gliding body with a long, whip-like tail. Wide wings wrap the face and taper into one sweeping temple.

Tone

Cobalt and amber. Fast and technical, with warmth where light passes through the lens.

Range

Gloss cobalt frame, amber lens, gold hinge hardware and embossed temple branding.
